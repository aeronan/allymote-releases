Allymote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Allymote is an iPhone app that lets you watch and drive the AI agents running in herdr on your own computers. This policy covers Allymote version 1.0.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. The developer of Allymote runs no server for the app and receives no data from it: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reports sent to us.
  • The app talks only to your own computers. Everything you see and send in Allymote travels between your iPhone and the computers you paired yourself, over your own network (normally your Tailscale network), encrypted with HTTPS.
  • What stays on your phone stays on your phone. Nothing the app keeps is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Who runs what

Allymote has two parts, and you run both of them:

  1. The app on your iPhone.
  2. The Allymote server on your own Mac or Windows PC, next to herdr. herdr is a separate, third-party product (herdr.dev); Allymote works with herdr but is not made by or affiliated with its developers.

There is no third part. The developer of Allymote does not operate a relay, a cloud service or any other server that your data passes through. Version 1.0 has no push notifications.

What the app sends, and where

The app connects only to the addresses of the computers you pair with it. It accepts only https addresses. Over that connection it sends and receives:

  • the agents, terminal screens and conversations that your computer shows it;
  • what you type, the keys you press, your answers to an agent's questions and the sessions you start;
  • photos and files you choose to attach (see below);
  • when pairing, the one-time pairing code and a device name of the form "Allymote on iPhone" (the name you gave your phone is not sent).

This traffic goes from your device to your own computer. It is not sent to the developer.

The setup guide in the app contains links to the Tailscale, herdr and Allymote download pages. Tapping one opens Safari; those sites have their own privacy policies.

What is kept on your phone

  • Device tokens, one per paired computer, in the iOS Keychain, readable only while the device is unlocked, on this device only (they are not synced to iCloud and are not restored to another device).
  • Settings and list preferences in the app's own storage on the phone: the paired computers' names and addresses, whether the app lock is on, text size and layout, pinned, muted and folded items in the list, and the first messages you used for new sessions (text that looks like a password or key is never kept).

Deleting the app removes its settings. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; if you install Allymote again, it deletes any token no paired computer uses when it is first unlocked. To remove a phone's access from a computer, revoke it in Settings → Paired Devices, or on the computer.

Camera

The app asks for the camera only when you choose to use it:

  • to scan the pairing QR code that your computer shows;
  • to take a photo when you choose Take Photo to attach it.

QR codes are read on the phone. A photo you take is sent only to the computer whose agent you are attaching it to. The app does not keep a copy and does not save it to your photo library.

Photos and files you attach

When you attach a photo (from the camera or your library) or a file, it is sent to your own computer, so the agent there can read it. Photos are sent without their location or other metadata. Your computer keeps attachments in the Allymote data folder (~/.allymote/uploads on a Mac, %LOCALAPPDATA%\allymote\uploads on Windows) and deletes them after 7 days. The app reads from your photo library only the items you pick in the system picker.

Face ID

Allymote locks itself when it opens and when you come back after more than a minute away, and unlocks with Face ID (or Touch ID, or your device passcode). The check is done by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded. Your face data never reaches the app, the developer or your computer. You can turn the lock off in Settings.

The demo

Try the Demo on the pairing screen shows a pretend computer whose content is built into the app. It works offline: nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is stored in the Keychain.

Your computer and your network

Your computer and your Tailscale network are yours. What the Allymote server and herdr on your computer keep (for example, its list of paired devices, an audit log of their actions, and attachments for 7 days) stays on that computer. Tailscale is a third-party service with its own privacy policy; Allymote does not send it anything beyond the normal network traffic between your own devices.

Children

Allymote is a tool for people who run AI coding agents on their own computers. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a later version changes what is described here (for example, by adding push notifications), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sidepathnotes@gmail.com

Allymote 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

Allymote 是一款 iPhone app，讓你查看並操作自己電腦上、在 herdr 裡執行的 AI agent。本政策適用於 Allymote 1.0 版。

重點摘要

  • 我們不蒐集任何資料。 Allymote 的開發者沒有為這個 app 架設任何伺服器，也不會從 app 收到任何資料：沒有帳號、沒有分析、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤，也不會把當機報告傳給我們。
  • App 只和你自己的電腦連線。 你在 Allymote 裡看到和送出的一切，都只在你的 iPhone 和你親自配對的電腦之間傳輸，經由你自己的網路（通常是你的 Tailscale 網路），並以 HTTPS 加密。
  • 留在手機上的就只留在手機上。 App 保存的任何東西都不會傳給開發者或其他任何人。

誰在執行什麼

Allymote 由兩個部分組成，兩者都由你自己執行：

  1. 你 iPhone 上的 app。
  2. 你自己的 Mac 或 Windows 電腦上、與 herdr 一起執行的 Allymote 伺服器。herdr 是獨立的第三方產品（herdr.dev）；Allymote 可與 herdr 搭配使用，但並非由 herdr 的開發者製作，與其也沒有任何隸屬關係。

除此之外沒有第三個部分。Allymote 的開發者不經營任何中繼站、雲端服務或其他會經手你資料的伺服器。1.0 版沒有推播通知。

App 會送出什麼、送到哪裡

App 只會連線到你配對的電腦位址，而且只接受 https 位址。透過這個連線，它會傳送與接收：

  • 你的電腦提供給它的 agent、終端機畫面和對話；
  • 你輸入的文字、按下的按鍵、你對 agent 問題的回答，以及你啟動的工作階段；
  • 你選擇附加的照片和檔案（見下文）；
  • 配對時的一次性配對碼，以及「Allymote on iPhone」這類的裝置名稱（你為手機取的名稱不會被送出）。

這些傳輸都是從你的裝置到你自己的電腦，不會傳給開發者。

App 內的設定指南附有 Tailscale、herdr 和 Allymote 下載頁面的連結。點選後會在 Safari 開啟；這些網站各有自己的隱私權政策。

保存在手機上的資料

  • 裝置權杖：每台配對的電腦一個，存放在 iOS 鑰匙圈中，只在裝置解鎖時可讀取，且只存在這台裝置上（不會同步到 iCloud，也不會還原到其他裝置）。
  • 設定與列表偏好：存放在手機上 app 自己的儲存空間，包括配對電腦的名稱與位址、是否開啟 app 鎖定、文字大小與版面，列表中釘選、靜音和收合的項目，以及你用過的新工作階段開頭訊息（看起來像密碼或金鑰的文字一律不保存）。

刪除 app 會一併刪除它的設定。iOS 可能在 app 刪除後保留鑰匙圈項目；若你重新安裝 Allymote，它會在第一次解鎖時刪除沒有任何配對電腦使用的權杖。若要撤銷某支手機對電腦的存取權，請在「設定」→「已配對的裝置」中撤銷，或在電腦上撤銷。

相機

App 只會在你選擇使用時才要求相機權限：

  • 掃描電腦顯示的配對 QR 碼；
  • 在你選擇「拍照」附加照片時拍攝照片。

QR 碼在手機上讀取。你拍攝的照片只會傳送到你要附加照片的那個 agent 所在的電腦。App 不會保留副本，也不會存入你的照片圖庫。

你附加的照片和檔案

當你附加照片（來自相機或照片圖庫）或檔案時，它會被傳送到你自己的電腦，讓那裡的 agent 讀取。照片送出時不含位置或其他中繼資料。你的電腦會把附件存放在 Allymote 資料夾（Mac 上為 ~/.allymote/uploads，Windows 上為 %LOCALAPPDATA%\allymote\uploads），並在 7 天後刪除。App 只會讀取你在系統選擇器中挑選的照片圖庫項目。

Face ID

Allymote 在開啟時，以及你離開超過一分鐘後回來時會自動鎖定，並以 Face ID（或 Touch ID、裝置密碼）解鎖。驗證由 iOS 執行，app 只會知道驗證是否成功。你的臉部資料永遠不會傳到 app、開發者或你的電腦。你可以在「設定」中關閉鎖定。

示範模式

配對畫面上的「試用示範」會顯示一台虛擬電腦，其內容都內建在 app 中。它可離線使用：不會傳送任何資料，也不會在鑰匙圈中儲存任何東西。

你的電腦與你的網路

你的電腦和你的 Tailscale 網路屬於你自己。你電腦上的 Allymote 伺服器和 herdr 所保存的資料（例如已配對裝置清單、裝置操作的稽核紀錄，以及保存 7 天的附件）都留在那台電腦上。Tailscale 是第三方服務，有自己的隱私權政策；除了你自己裝置之間的一般網路傳輸外，Allymote 不會傳送任何資料給它。

兒童

Allymote 是給在自己電腦上執行 AI 程式 agent 的人使用的工具，並非以兒童為對象，也不會蒐集任何人的資料。

政策變更

若之後的版本改變了本政策所描述的內容（例如加入推播通知），本政策會在該版本發布前更新，並標示新的生效日期。
